# Product Vision

## Current Audit Note - 2026-06-14

Aarast / اعّرست remains a Wedding Financial OS + Marketplace product, with the planning and financial system as the center of gravity. The current codebase now includes the backend provider-reference marketplace, provider dashboard, admin provider approval workflow, and Flutter marketplace screens, but these are still planning/reference and quote-lead flows only.

This does not change the product boundary: payments, confirmed bookings, deposits, loans, credit, financing, production AI, and internal chat remain out of scope until explicitly approved.

## What Aarast Is

Aarast / اعّرست is a mobile app for Yemen that helps people planning marriage manage the full wedding process from early planning through wedding-day readiness. It is designed for the financial and coordination reality of weddings, where the groom, bride, families, providers, and installment obligations often need to be tracked together.

## Problem It Solves

Wedding planning is usually scattered across notebooks, WhatsApp chats, informal promises, vendor visits, payment reminders, and family conversations. This makes it hard to know:

- How much the wedding is expected to cost.
- What has already been paid.
- What is still owed.
- Which debts or installments are due soon.
- Which tasks are assigned to which person.
- Which providers or quotes are still being compared.
- Whether the plan is affordable before commitments are made.

Aarast gives couples and families a single planning system for wedding money, tasks, dates, and later vendor discovery.

## Why Yemen

Yemen has strong social and family wedding traditions, often with complex shared responsibilities and informal financial commitments. Many people need practical planning support before they need a marketplace. A product built for Yemen should respect local cities, local provider categories, Arabic-first usage, family participation, and budget sensitivity.

## Why It Is Not Just A Wedding Marketplace

A marketplace alone helps users find vendors, but it does not solve the harder planning problem: deciding what is affordable, tracking what has been paid, managing debt, coordinating family responsibilities, and staying on schedule.

Aarast should not start by becoming only a directory of halls, photographers, salons, and services. Vendor discovery is useful, but it becomes more valuable when connected to a real wedding plan, budget, quotes, obligations, and tasks.

## Privacy Boundaries

Shared planning must respect privacy boundaries between the groom, bride, and family members. Not every participant should automatically see every financial detail, debt, installment, or private task. Aarast should support collaboration while keeping sensitive wedding-plan information visible only to the people who need it.

## Core Positioning

Aarast should grow in this order:

1. Wedding Financial OS: budget, categories, expenses, debts, installments, tasks, timeline, and shared planning.
2. Marketplace: vendors, services, packages, reviews, favorites, quote requests, and booking leads only in early stages, without payment processing or confirmed deposits in the MVP.
3. AI Planning: suggestions, reminders, planning assistance, affordability guidance, and vendor matching.
4. FinTech Partnerships: future payment, installment, savings, or financing integrations only after the core planning product proves useful.
