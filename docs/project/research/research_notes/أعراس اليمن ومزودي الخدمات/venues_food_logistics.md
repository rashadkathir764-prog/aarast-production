# Yemeni wedding vendors: venue, food and logistics (halls, tents, cooks/slaughter, water, security, cars, chairs/sound/generators)

Research date: 2026-10-19. About 17 web searches and fetches. Open-web coverage of Yemeni wedding vendors is **thin**. Most real vendor activity runs on Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok and phone, and the search and fetch tools could not index or open those. Facebook pages could not be fetched, and one Yemen Mazad category page returned HTTP 403. Every fact below has a source. Lines marked **[GUESS]** are inferences, not sourced facts.

---

## 1. How each vendor is booked (channel, deposit/عربون, contract)

### Takeaway
I found **no published source** on deposit (عربون) amounts, refund rules or written contracts for any Yemeni wedding vendor. What is documented: booking happens by direct contact (phone or WhatsApp, "call for price"). Two directory/booking platforms exist in Sana'a: Monasabat (مناسبات) and Halm Al-Asima (حلم العاصمة). In Houthi-controlled areas, halls and event vendors also need **prior permits** from the authorities.

### Cited Findings
- Monasabat (monasabatyemeniah.com) is a Yemeni web and mobile app. Users browse vendors in these categories: wedding halls, rest houses (استراحات), photography, bouquets, nasheed singers, DJs, event organizing, wedding stages (كوشات), food and cakes, and lighting. The user picks an item, sees its price and sends a **booking request for the event date**. The page says nothing about deposits or payment terms. Contact is by email, phone, WhatsApp and social media, and the app is on Google Play and the App Store. Footer © 2026. — [Monasabat koshah page](https://monasabatyemeniah.com/services/koshah)
- Halm Al-Asima (halmalasima.com) is a Sana'a directory. It claims "300+ halls in Sana'a" and over 1,000 providers. Its categories include halls, kosha, tables and chairs, meals, bakeries, "water and drinks" (مياه ومشروبات), buffets, cakes, event equipment, DJs and bands, **security teams (فرق أمن)**, and photographers. It has **no separate category for tents, cooks or cars**. The "book now" and "call now" buttons led to placeholder pages, and the only contact listed was WhatsApp +967775253244. It advertises a 5% discount for booking through the site. — [Halm Al-Asima](https://www.halmalasima.com/)
- On a Yemeni services marketplace, most halls are listed with "call us to know the price" (اتصل بنا لمعرفة السعر). Source is a search-result summary; the domain yemenss.com now redirects to a domain-sale page. — [yemenss.com (search snippet)](https://www.yemenss.com/parties-events/wedding-halls/)
- A circular from the Houthi "Ministry of Culture and Tourism" requires **wedding hall owners, event contractors, art troupes and photographers to get prior permits**. It also subjects hall bookings to **fixed fees** (no amounts given), bans fireworks, shortens celebrations, and lets only ministry-approved photographers work. The article is roughly 2025; the page shows only "a year ago". — [Al-Sahil](https://www.alsahil.net/news34482.html)
- Al-Quds Al-Arabi also reports that holding weddings and events in Sana'a requires a prior licence (ترخيص مسبق). — [Al-Quds Al-Arabi](https://www.alquds.co.uk/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ad%d9%88%d8%ab%d9%8a%d9%88%d9%86-%d9%8a%d8%b4%d8%aa%d8%b1%d8%b7%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%aa%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%ae%d9%8a%d8%b5-%d9%85%d8%b3%d8%a8%d9%82%d8%a9-%d9%84%d8%a5%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%85%d8%a9/)
- One Sana'a car-rental office advertises booking by phone: "for booking 775225894". — [Saber Yemen listing](https://saber-yemen.com/product/10670)

### Inferences
- [GUESS] Booking is mostly by phone or WhatsApp, or in person for halls, with a cash عربون. Contracts are probably verbal or a simple receipt (سند). This is a common regional pattern; no source here confirms it for Yemen.
- The permit requirement in Houthi areas means a hall booking in Sana'a may depend on an external approval step. The app could model this as a "permit obtained" checklist item or status for hall bookings in Sana'a, but not for Aden.

### Gaps
- Deposit size (fixed amount or % of the price), whether it is refundable, cancellation and postponement rules: **no source found** for any vendor type.
- Whether halls issue written contracts: no source found.
- The amount of the Houthi "fixed fees" on hall bookings: not given in any source.

## 2. Pricing models and example prices

### Takeaway
Women's halls in Sana'a quote **packages in USD**: about $1,200–$2,000, including about 300 dinner meals, video coverage and an all-female band; a premium hall is over $3,000 (2026). Aden hall rent was quoted as at least 400,000 YER in 2020. Some vendors price in foreign currency because the rial collapsed. The wedding lunch (غداء) is a major cost: over 2 million YER in Abyan in 2023. Stage (kosha) and tent rentals appear priced per event. Rental cars are priced per day, not per hour.

### Cited Findings
**Halls**
- Sana'a women's event hall, manager Nabil Zakhaim: a wedding reception costs **$1,200 to $2,000**, including about **300 dinner meals**, coverage "from the bride's arrival to the end", and an all-female band. Pre-arranged "wedding scenario" programs cost at least **$150** extra. The hall holds about **6 weddings per month**. A bride said "Lavender" hall costs **over $3,000**. Social estimates put the total wedding at about **10 million YER (about $20,000)**. The article has no explicit date; image paths suggest about February 2026. — [Al-Mahriah](https://almahriah.net/reports/poverty-extravagance-how-sana-as-weddings-became-a-burden)
- A new Sana'a hall with an indoor hall plus an outdoor poolside hall rents for about **$1,500 (about 1 million YER at 595 YER/USD)**. Undated; the 595 rate suggests about 2019–2020 in Sana'a old rial. — [Mazpress](https://mazpress.com/news7981.html)
- A social-media post says some Sana'a halls rent for up to 1,000,000 YER. Undated. — [Threads post](https://www.threads.com/@hakem_alhakimi/post/DZNdHuGD_Wm/)
- Aden: hall rent is "**not less than 400,000 YER**". Women's-hall extras are each charged separately: changing the kosha, extra lighting and laser lighting, additional female staff, additional rooms or halls, nuts and sweets on tables, and a "dinner fee" (مبلغ العشاء). "Some halls, cake makers… and singers now charge in **foreign currency**." Dated 7 Sep 2020. — [Aden Time](https://www.aden-tm.net/news/143867)
- A 2014 report gives hall prices of about 150,000 YER, and 250,000–300,000 for a women's hall. It is old and the city is unclear. — [Al-Raya](https://www.raya.com/2014/01/27/%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B3-%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%84-300-%D8%A3%D9%84%D9%81-%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84/amp)

**Kosha / decor**
- Sana'a kosha vendor "مكتب حفله متعهد حفلات | كوش اعراس" (Al-Wahda district) lists **2,380,000 YER**. "كوش هيڤن" (Al-Daery) shows no price. 2026 listing. — [Monasabat](https://monasabatyemeniah.com/services/koshah)

**Food / lunch / slaughter**
- Central Abyan (Lawdar, al-Ain): wedding **lunch costs "sometimes exceed 2 million rials"**. Slaughter animals (ذبائح) are listed among demands from the bride's family. Dated 16 Nov 2023 (new rial area). — [Yemen Future](https://yemenfuture.net/researches/18099)
- Hujariya (Taiz) tradition: a bull is slaughtered for a lunch for the bride's family; the source says the custom is fading. — [Al-Ayyam (search summary)](https://www.alayyam.info/news/5ZED0TZO-MR3S59)
- Livestock price proxies: sacrificial animals in Yemen cost **$100–300** (Jun 2025). — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.net/ebusiness/2025/6/1/sacrificial-animal-prices-2025-arab-countries). Imported livestock costs 170,000–190,000 YER. — [Al-Ain](https://al-ain.com/article/eid-aladha-yemen-sacrifices-prices). Prices jumped 40%. — [Al-Araby](https://www.alaraby.co.uk/economy/%D8%A3%D8%B6%D8%A7%D8%AD%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%87-%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D9%82%D9%81%D8%B2-40)
- A 2024 mass wedding slaughtered about 120 bulls for traditional food. — [search summary of Al-Watan/related report](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1037142)

**Tents / majlis / chairs**
- Yemen Mazad's "halls and tents" services category lists wedding tents and majlis seating for rent at about **100,000–300,000 YER**, plus hall booking in al-Hawban (Taiz). This comes from a search summary; the page itself returned 403. — [Yemen Mazad halls & tents](https://yemenmazad.com/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D9%8A%D9%85)
- OpenSooq ad: "5 wedding tents (مخيمات اعراس) of different sizes for sale due to sudden travel", one quoted at 1,000,000 YER. This shows that individuals own tent inventory in several sizes. — [OpenSooq](https://ye.opensooq.com/ar/search/260727777)

**Cars**
- Yemen Car Rental, Sana'a: an economy car costs about **20,000–30,000 YER/day (about $80–120)** and an SUV about $160–240/day, with 30% off monthly. — [carsrental.com.ye](https://carsrental.com.ye/)
- Yemen Al-Hadarat office, Sana'a: advertises luxury cars "for a friend's or relative's wedding" at **150 SAR**, with 20% off weekly and 30% off monthly. — [Sofargle listing](https://sofargle.com/%D9%85%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A8-%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%8A%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%B6%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-1967)
- In Aden, car rental **and decoration** (تزيين) are listed as separate wedding cost items. — [Aden Time](https://www.aden-tm.net/news/143867)

### Inferences
- The pricing units seen are: **per event package** (women's hall with meals included), **per event rental** (kosha, tent), **per day** (cars), **lump sum** (lunch), and **per animal** (livestock bought at market prices). No source shows per-plate (بالصحن) or per-head (بالرأس) pricing, but the "300 dinner meals included" detail implies that capacity and meal count define the hall package.
- Currency is mixed: USD (Sana'a halls), SAR (cars), old YER (Sana'a) and new YER (Aden/Abyan). The app needs currency and the rial zone on every price. A Sana'a price of "1,000,000 YER" and an Aden price of "1,000,000 YER" are very different amounts: around 2019–2020 the rate was about 595/USD in Sana'a, and since 2021 the Aden rial has been much weaker.
- [GUESS] Wedding car pricing is probably a flat price per zaffa trip with a driver, plus decoration. No source confirms per-hour pricing.

### Gaps
- Per-plate or per-guest prices from kitchens or restaurants (مطابخ/مطاعم), and cook fees per sheep (أجرة الطباخ على الذبيحة): **no source found**.
- Afternoon versus night (عصر/ليل) period pricing for halls: not found.
- Security guard pricing (per guard or per night): not found.
- Water and water-cooler rental pricing (ثلاجات/برادات الماء): not found at all.
- Sound system and generator rental prices for weddings: not found; only generic generator sale listings exist on OpenSooq.

## 3. Capacity and double booking

### Takeaway
I found almost no direct evidence. One Sana'a women's hall does about 6 weddings a month. Some venues have **more than one hall**: indoor plus outdoor, and Aden halls rent "additional rooms/halls". Tent owners hold several tents of different sizes. Car offices have fleets. I found no evidence of how vendors prevent double booking today.

### Cited Findings
- About 6 weddings per month at one Sana'a women's hall. — [Al-Mahriah](https://almahriah.net/reports/poverty-extravagance-how-sana-as-weddings-became-a-burden)
- A new Sana'a venue has two spaces, an enclosed hall and an outdoor poolside hall. — [Mazpress](https://mazpress.com/news7981.html)
- Aden halls charge separately for additional rooms or halls, and families often must rent a larger hall than their guest count needs. — [Aden Time](https://www.aden-tm.net/news/143867)
- One tent owner sells 5 wedding tents of various sizes. — [OpenSooq](https://ye.opensooq.com/ar/search/260727777)
- The large Sana'a hall "Thu Raydan" (ذو ريدان) is listed with a 2,000-person capacity. — [Halm Al-Asima directory page](https://halmalasimaa.github.io/yemen/halm/a/99/99.html)

### Inferences
- The app should model a hall vendor with **multiple bookable spaces** (sub-halls, indoor/outdoor), each with a capacity, and possibly time slots. Tent, car and chair vendors should be modelled as **inventory-based** (N units of type X), not as one resource per date.
- [GUESS] Today double booking is avoided with a paper or WhatsApp diary kept by the owner. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- Kitchen daily capacity (how many weddings or sheep per day), and security-company headcount: no source.
- Whether halls run two events per day (afternoon women's and evening): not found.

## 4. Men's vs women's events

### Takeaway
Women's weddings happen in **halls (صالات/قاعات) or tents (مخيمات)** with a kosha, band, photography and dinner. Men's celebrations are not covered in the halls sources. Houthi rules restrict women's hall events: an 8–9 pm end time, no loudspeakers, the groom may not enter to collect the bride, no photographing the bride, and no hired female singers in some districts.

### Cited Findings
- A women's hall package includes about 300 dinner meals, an all-female band, and coverage from the bride's arrival to the end. — [Al-Mahriah](https://almahriah.net/reports/poverty-extravagance-how-sana-as-weddings-became-a-burden)
- Aden women's halls: kosha changes, lighting and laser lighting, extra female staff, nuts and sweets on tables, buffets, fast food, a dinner fee. In the past "a simple bottle of juice" was served. — [Aden Time](https://www.aden-tm.net/news/143867)
- Houthi circular (Hamdan district, Sana'a, about 2024): women's weddings in **halls and tents** end at **8 pm, 9 pm at most**. It bans loudspeakers, bans the groom entering the hall to take the bride, bans photographing the bride and bans inviting female artists. Fines go up to **500,000 YER**. — [Al-Masdar Online](https://almasdaronline.com/articles/303821); [Barran Press](https://barran.press/news/5832); [Yemen Future](https://yemenfuture.net/news/26609); [Al-Mawqea Post](https://almawqeapost.net/news/104199)
- Men may not be photographed in halls reserved for women, and only ministry-approved photographers may work. — [Al-Sahil](https://www.alsahil.net/news34482.html)

### Inferences
- The women's event is the main hall product. Men's gatherings (qat chew, lunch, zaffa) are likely at a men's hall, a tent, or the home. [GUESS, needs field confirmation.]
- The app should treat a hall booking's gender (رجال/نساء) and the end time as first-class fields. In Sana'a, a regulatory end time applies to women's events.

### Gaps
- Explicit sources on men's hall pricing and what men's halls provide (water, qat-session seating, lunch): not found.

## 5. Typical problems

### Takeaway
Documented problems: cost escalation and debt, foreign-currency pricing, extras charged separately (price creep), and the regulatory risk of fines and restrictions in Houthi areas. Power cuts are a well-known national problem, but I found no wedding-specific source. I found **no source** on double booking, late arrival or deposit disputes.

### Cited Findings
- Extras are charged separately: kosha change, lighting, staff, extra rooms, sweets. Families must rent bigger halls than needed. Vendors moved to foreign-currency pricing. Weddings are financed by bank loans and rotating savings groups (هكبات). — [Aden Time, 2020](https://www.aden-tm.net/news/143867)
- Fines up to 500,000 YER for breaking Houthi wedding rules; citizens describe the measures as a means of revenue extraction. — [Al-Masdar Online](https://almasdaronline.com/articles/303821); [Al-Sahil](https://www.alsahil.net/news34482.html)
- Power context: Saudi-funded emergency generators (200 MW) were sent to Mukalla and Seiyun as a temporary fix, showing chronic shortages in the south. Jul 2026. — [Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/arab-and-world/yemen/2026/07/01/%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%88%D9%84%D9%89-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D9%83%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%AD%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%85%D9%88%D8%AA)
- Generators are sold and rented through OpenSooq Yemen. — [OpenSooq generators](https://ye.opensooq.com/ar/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B5%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D9%85%D9%88%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%83%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%A1)

### Inferences
- [GUESS] Whether the hall includes a generator, or whether one is an extra, is probably a key question for customers, given grid outages. The app could add an "includes generator/backup power" attribute.
- Fixing the price at booking matters, given currency swings and extras. The app should record the agreed currency, the total and an itemized list of included and extra services.

### Gaps
- Documented cases of double booking, vendor no-shows, late cooks or cars, or deposit disputes: none found online. This needs field interviews or Facebook group mining.
